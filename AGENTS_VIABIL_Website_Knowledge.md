# VIABIL Website Knowledge

## Company Context

VIABIL is a Brazilian B2B software company focused on financial feasibility, investment analysis, landbank control, and performance monitoring for real estate development projects.

The company is recognized in the Brazilian real estate development market as a reference in viabilidade financeira imobiliária. It is not a generic SaaS product. It is a specialized vertical platform built around the financial, operational, legal, and market variables that influence real estate development decisions in Brazil.

VIABIL serves incorporadoras, construtoras, developers, real estate funds, private equity firms, investors, consultants, and companies involved in the decision-making process of real estate projects.

The platform supports decisions from the earliest stage of a project, especially land acquisition and business origination, through feasibility analysis, scenario simulation, project approval, monitoring of planned versus actual results, and route correction/replanning.

VIABIL should be presented as a mature, trusted, market-tested solution that is becoming more modern, more complete, more data-driven, and more connected to the current speed of the real estate market.

Do not describe VIABIL as a startup. Do not make it sound like a new, unproven product. It has history, authority, and market recognition.

## Current Market Perception

VIABIL is already known for:

* Credibility in real estate financial feasibility.
* Deep technical knowledge of the Brazilian real estate development market.
* Strong adherence to real market variables.
* Use by companies that need serious financial decision support.
* Training and qualification of market professionals.
* Practical knowledge accumulated through daily discussion of real cases.

The website should preserve this trust while making the company feel more contemporary, digital, clear, and strategic.

The website should communicate that VIABIL is alive, updated, active, and stronger than ever.

## What VIABIL Wants the Website to Show

The website should show that VIABIL is no longer only a software for initial feasibility analysis.

The website should show VIABIL as a complete financial intelligence solution for the real estate development cycle.

The key message is:

> VIABIL is the reference in financial feasibility for real estate development, now in a more modern, complete, updated, and integrated phase.

The website should make it obvious that VIABIL helps clients across the full journey:

1. Capturing business opportunities.
2. Organizing and evaluating land opportunities.
3. Running feasibility studies.
4. Simulating scenarios.
5. Supporting investment decisions.
6. Monitoring planned versus actual performance.
7. Replanning routes when reality changes.
8. Keeping financial assumptions updated with market, tax, legal, and regulatory changes.

The website should especially speak to CEOs, founders, and C-level decision-makers at incorporadoras.
